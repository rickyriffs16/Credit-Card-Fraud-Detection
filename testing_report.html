1 Test Objectives & Scope

This report documents the rigorous empirical testing and functional validation performed on the **Credit Card Fraud Detection System**. Testing was structured across three primary dimensions: (1) **Algorithmic Evaluation** against extreme 0.17% class imbalance; (2) **Functional Quality Assurance** of authentication, session persistence, and UI; and (3) **Performance Benchmarking** measuring latency and batch throughput.

Commercial Banking Significance of False Positive Control

While Logistic Regression caught 90 out of 98 frauds, it triggered **1,458 false alarms**, which in production would block innocent customer credit cards and overwhelm manual investigation queues. Random Forest reduced false alarms by **97.3% (down to 40)** while preserving high detection recall.
